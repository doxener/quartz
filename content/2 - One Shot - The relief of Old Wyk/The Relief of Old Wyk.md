## Chapter 1 - Exposition
[[Hadarac]] has become relentless, even though it is mostly over seas, [[Tamor]] is at war. Since the fall of [[Grallo]], [[Hadarac]] has turned its blade towards East-[[Tamor]]. The Eremon passage, recently better known as the crows passage, has become the battleground for the clashing between the two most powerful countries of Irrida. 

Within this battlefield there are a few important fortresses and towns that limit the advance of [[Hadarac]]. Most notably the large town of [[Old Wyk]]. This well defendable town has become the key to crossing the passage. It marks the most southern point of [[Tamor]] control. However for the past seven and a half months [[Old Wyk]] has been under siege. It has held out for this long while the main focus of [[Hadarac]] was on [[Grallo]]. Since Grallo's defeat there have been sightings of an immense warband gathering in [[Xarrakir]]. 

As a reaction [[Tamor]] has decided to gather the brunt of their garrison in [[Ridgeview]]. They need to relief [[Old Wyk]] from the siege and retake control over the passage if they want any chance of stopping this sighted warband. This relief however has proven itself to be more challenging than initially thought. 

With this shift of focus, [[Hadarac]] has sent almost all of its [[wargs]] to the crows passage. This means they know of everything that [[Tamor]] is doing. Even more importantly this allowed them to completely cut off any communication to and from [[Old Wyk]]. Any messengers leaving [[Ridgeview]] or other encampments is intercepted and never received. 

The besieging forces of [[Hadarac]] have had so much time that they have build their own fortifications completely surrounding the town. This makes a straightforward relief to risky and they need to coordinate a sortie from the garrison in [[Old Wyk]]. So it is crucial that they get a message across to The Candle.

Time is running short for [[Tamor]] as the warband in [[Xarrakir]] is growing in size and soon will be ready. Furthermore general Raltara has finally gotten approval for the usage of two elite sky guards who will soon arrive from [[Ravenport]]. Their limited availability has put even more strain on the time available for the relief of [[Old Wyk]].

In a last hope of getting the message across to the candle, Raltara has asked [[The Silver Compass]] to send five qualified parties of adventurers to [[Ridgeview]]. It is the task of these adventurers to deliver the message to [[Old Wyk]].

## Chapter 2 - Arrival in Ridgeview

![[Ridgeview impressions.jpg]]

Limb-bane has personally asked the five of you to represent the [[Silver Compass]] as one of the parties requested by general Raltara. All of you know that these are the kind of missions that will launch you into fame across [[Tamor]] and [[Irrida]]. After being received at the gate the party will be pointed towards their temporary home for their short stay in the city. When they are complete, they will go towards general Raltara's war council to discuss the details. After which they will have a the rest of the day to prepare for they ride out at first light the next day. 

#### A temporary home 
An abandoned merchant house has been appointed as their lodging for this day. Here the adventurers will meet (again) and stay to prepare for their important task. 

#### The Briefing
Imbedded in the ridge, the castle of [[Ridgeview]] houses the war council set up by general Raltara. The council is made up by himself, general Ernesto D'Medici from [[Picta]] and ten other advisors. Notable members are the lord of [[Ridgeview]], Lerke Montlor, arcanist Ornat Nilam and greathunter Ferrick Nortt from the hunters guild.

General Raltara will receive the adventurers and lay out the important points:

- Old Wyk needs to be relieved and then resupplied.
- Time is running low. On the fourth dawn from now, we will advance.
- They need to coordinate a sortie. Thus they need to send a message.
- Wargs are watching everything so they need alternative ways.
- The attack strategy

The adventurers will be the second group and they will approach through the [[Whisperstone Mountains]]. They will make use of and old and abandoned mine that runs through the Whisperstone mountains. From there they will just have to follow the whisp to arrive at Old Wyk. 

#### The Preparation

They are expected the next day at first light at the lower eastern gate. Where they will travel the first leg of the journey on the wagon of Rembrandt, the hides merchant.

## Chapter 3 - The first day of travel

#### Travel to the mountains
The first leg is quite uneventful, after a few hours the adventurers will be dropped at the foot of the whisperstone mountains to continue on game trails. 

The trails are quite overgrown but still reasonably safe. How higher they get the less dense the greenery and more dangerous the trail. Many loose rocks are scattered around and the trail is surrounded by thick thorny red purple moorlands, ferns and an occasional pine tree.

One part of the trail has slowly broken of the mountain and the trail is gone for a section across a mountain. "de losse stenen lijken groter te worden en uiteindelijk maken ze plaats voor grote blokken die onderdeel zijn van de berg die jullie aan het trostseren zijn. Alleen net voor een lichte bocht in de rotswand naast jullie lijkt het pad waar jullie van gebruik maken op te houden. Alleen een richel blijft over waar ooit het pad liep. zo'n 30ft verder lijkt het pad weer verder te gaan."

#### Basilisks
In between two slopes where the trail has become wider and more surrounded by shrubs and greenery a DC 14 perception check will reveal the overgrown petrified arm of a hunter. Beneath the shrubs lies a crumbled 'statue' of a hunter. Further investigation in the surrounding area will reveal two more statues, one of which is fully in tact. This statue can be restored by the scroll of greater restoration to free [[Almar Kreston]] a lvl 6 human ranger hunter. Almar is willing to help the adventurers, at least in crossing the mountains, and can reveal the dangers lurking in the moors.

Then the first basilisk will show itself. Not long afterwards the second one will join the fight. During the fight a successful perception or investigation into the surroundings might reveal a reflective shield used by an unfortunate soul. 

#### Setting camp for the night
The mountains although dangerous are full of beautiful nature. The rain starts pouring later in the day but will stop once night has fallen. Shelter can be found all around, caves and stone overhangs or large trees and shrubberies. 

Making a fire is ill advised since it will draw the attention of the [[Hadarac]] wargs. The night will be a peaceful moment under the clear cold sky. The watch can spot some wildlife and admire the stars.

Either way they will encounter the warg and its sentry who have set up camp under a lagged overhang. The warg has control over a giant eagle and a large dire wolf, the wolf will be unnoticed at first unless they try to approach from the rock above.

#### The Northern Entrance 
A small outpost on the norther side of the mountain seems completely decrepit. Three wooden buildings surrounded by trees are now mostly rubble with a few sections still standing. Positioned in the mountainside is an opening to a dark tunnel. This is the locations as described during the briefing. 

The tunnel leads deep into the mountain and branches out into different mineshafts. The end of the tunnel is sealed by the collapsed rubble that can be cleared with the scroll of telekinesis. The tunnel leads into the abandoned mine section of the whisperstone caverns.

Apart from patrolling the region, their fortified position has appointed them as the guards of Hadarac's norther powder reserves. An immense stash of 8 barrels of [[Treonian Powder]] are stored inside the armoury.

## Chapter 5 - Getting inside 
#### The road to Old Wyk
Now in possession of the patrol routes the adventurers can make fast progress. They can ride through the light forests and eventually follow the whisp towards the town. Once they get closer they will have to slow down again and avoid patrols from the besiegers, including wargs. 

Depending on the their speed they will arrive at the start or end of the afternoon at the edge of the forest bordering the northern side of Old Wyk. The adventurers can take the time to comprise their plan of entry. Once inside the magic seal they can use the bell of silent alarm to reach the arcanist and speak the phrase "winds are rising in the west" to proof their allegiance.

#### Crossing the Contravallation
At nightfall the adventurers will have the most luck at crossing. They must find a way to cross the ditch climb the palisade guarded by **two orc deadeyes**. Afterwards they must avoid the **small patrols of line breakers and deadeyes**. Reinforcement (mostly deathseekers) will arrive if they are too slow and the orcs get a chance to call for them. Distractions can help if stealth fails, contacting Serrion Lerma will also help and provide a way inside. 

When crossing the contravallation they will have a chance to see one of the shaman totems that is used to uphold the magic seal. They can see a large piece of [[Elements from the rift#Cinderstone|Cinderstone]] being used as a conduit for dampening. Chained to the totem are multiple prisoners trapped by chains leading to a sharp barbed stake that has pierced the chest of the prisoners.

![[The_Contravellation.jpg]]

#### A spark of hope
The town seems sullen and on its last stand. The walls have been reinforced with hoardings and a few canoe's are stored inside of the walls next to the destroyed harbour. Some houses have been broken down to make these and the wall reinforcements. The garrison consists of 600 soldiers and 200 injured or sick. There are still 500 citizens within the walls. 

There are scarce looking store rooms and a small amount of livestock is kept in the town park. People are curious but tired, but after a while they start to get hope once they hear that there is news from the outside. 

The adventurers meet with the leaders of Old Wyk inside the candle. The large circular donjon with the famous high hall. Lord Erris Vars is technically in charge. He is the the ancestral lord of Old Wyk, he is still proud but tired. Old man Tom is the leader of the garrison. Tom is officially retired but after the death of commander Joran he took his place in the chain of command. He is unbreakable and rules with an iron fist. He also is the only reason Old Wyk has kept out for so long. Arcanist Serrion Lerma is one of the mages sent to Old Wyk at the start of the war together with a few appretices. They have been busy in  protecting the town from the magic of the shamans.

The adventurers can bring the news from [[Ridgeview]] and talk over the strategy for the sortie in the morning...

## Chapter 6 - The Relief
Shrouded by the darkness of the morning and the mist conjured by Serrion will the sortie of 300 man leave from the northern side of the town. The troops will leave in small groups (or canoes) and hide themself in the ruins of the harbour. Once the attack on the south side begins and Ernesto starts the northern assault will the sortie split up in three groups heading for the northern blockade. 

The adventurers will join a group of 100 soldiers in taking control over the left tower. While two other groups of 100 take on the right tower and the pontoon bridge. Their specific task is to neutralize- or seize control over the large ballista on top of the tower. They will find an **orc eye of gruumsh** in command and **6 death seekers.** 

#### Conclusion
A successful assault on the northern blockade will result in a well coordinated relief with limited casualties on the side of [[Tamor]]. Some of the forces from [[Hadarac]] might escape but [[Tamor]] will regain control over the passage and reinforce their defences for the eventual arrival of the warband from [[Xarrakir]].

The adventurers will go down in history as legends and saviours and become one of the most renowned adventuring groups of [[Tamor]]. This story is only one of their exploits but the one that started it all and is favoured under the bards and minstrels of [[Irrida]].
