# Ephyrea
Ephyrea is the name of the planet were our story takes place. It is located within [[The Pithia System]], and houses all the known civilizations in this system. This world, also known as the shattered planet is dominated by the presence of the [[The rift of Thessaly]], causing this world to be ruled by its currents and corruptions.

### History
$\quad$ *main page: [[History of Ephyrea]]*
The events that happened on Ephyrea can be divided into four distinct ages: The Illumination, The age of ascendance, The age of the scattered dawn and the era of Coalition. The first two ages are long lost to time and not much is know about them, mostly that they ended with the astrological catastrophe known as [[The Shattering]]. In the ages after the shattering, Ephyrea was separated by [[The rift of Thessaly]] into the ==continents== we now know. Our story takes place in the second age after [[The Shattering]] where the [[E.R.C.]] has been established and connected the continents.

### Calendar
$\quad$ *main page: [[The Ephyrean calendar]]*
The calendar in Ephyrea is based on the planetary cycles. One cycle takes 105 years. A year lasts 384 days, divided into 8 months, each lasting 12 weeks. The 4 days of the week are called: Brigthday, Duskday, Darkday and Dawnday. The days are 24 hours long, weeks are a 4 day cycle, a month is 12 weeks and a year is 8 moths, one cycle is 105 years. The daily cycle creates a time difference between the continents of 1 day and on the continents themselves the difference bridges this gap.

### Geographic characteristics
Life on Ephyrea is only possible because of [[The rift of Thessaly|the rift]], its darkness and circulations have created unique atmospheres around each continent. However the atmosphere being surrounded by darkness has caused light to be scarce. On Brigthday there is a hint of yellow and orange light, but mostly light is limited to the colours found during twilight. The terrain on Ephyrea can vary enormous, from hostile deserts and frozen wastelands to tropical coasts and fertile meadows.

#### Continents
[[The rift of Thessaly]] has physically separated the continents of Ephyrea, the large masses of land, remnants of an older whole planet, that remain suspended in a balance of darkness. The separation of these continents has for a long time meant that intercontinental trade, travel or connection was impossible. Since the creation of the [[E.R.C.]] intercontinental travel has been made possible, resulting in enormous growth and wealth. The four continents of Ephyrea are:

##### Lothria
$\quad$ *main page: [[Lothria]]*
Lothria is the smallest of the four continents but is considered the most important by some. Most notably Lothria houses the largest city in Ephyrea: [[Dras-Leona]] the capital of [[Treonia]]and the headquarters of the [[E.R.C.]] combined with the centre of intercontinental travel: [[The Monolith]]. Lothria also is home to the largest ruins from before [[The Shattering]]: The [[I'nir Ruins]]. The realms of Lothria are [[Y'Dris]], [[Treonia]], [[Fyor-Grad]] and [[Albrona]].

##### Irrida
$\quad$ *main page: [[Irrida]]*
Irrida is the second largest continent but the one with the most inhabitants. Irrida is mostly known for its general produce and mercenary's/adventurers. But recently the realms have been gathering their own forces for the threat of war has become all to real with [[Hadarac]]'s relentless advance. Rumours are going spreading that the small realm of [[Anor]] has made incredible process in the field of science and that they might even know more about travel in the rift then the [[E.R.C.]]. The realms of Irrida are: the kingdom of [[Tamor]], the republic of [[Anor]], The waste of [[Hadarac]], [[Picta]], [[Grallo]], [[Olron]], [[Vroengard]] and [[Ineria]].

##### Thera
$\quad$ *main page: [[Thera]]*
Thera is slightly smaller as [[Irrida]] and is known mostly for [[The Nelorian Empire]]. The empire has been trying to unify the whole continent under one banner and has almost succeeded. The last straggler [[Nixia]], was considered to have no chance against the empire. But [[Nixia]] has proven otherwise, they responded fiercely and since been a thorn in their side. Thera is also home to 3 dark drifts which have made the Therians early experimenters of tide travel. The realms of Thera are: [[Nelore]], [[Nixia]], [[Erindor]], [[Ravinaz]], [[Cartona]], [[Caldwen]], [[Trailla]] and [[Romere]].

##### Kovir
$\quad$ *main page: [[Kovir]]*
Kovir is the largest continent of Ephyrea but is home to the least amount of people. Kovir only has three realms. [[Cindore]], [[Galdron]] and [[Laonia]]. Cindore is an influential realm just because of one thing, the [[Ithral academy]] for the arcane. The frozen realm of Galdron is a harsh place, but considered one of the backbones of society. Almost all of the [[Elements from the rift#Stygium|Stygium]] used in rift travel is harvested on the Galdron outskirts. It is brutal work but there is money and importance to it. The last realm of Kovir is the largest piece of unexplored land on Ephyrea. Many legends tell of the countless dangers, and most sane people are wise enough not to enter.  

#### Taranost
$\quad$ *main page: [[Taranost]]*
Taranost, also known as the realms below, is a vast subterranean region that exists below the continents of Ephyrea. It is a dark and hostile place, where [[The Corruption]] is embedded in the very stone. The Underdark is almost a world unto itself, with civilizations, monsters, politics, economies, and threats that may interact only sporadically or indirectly with the surface world. [[The Corruption]] mutates any life that survives down there and The Corrupted also seem to seek out these even darker lands.

### Seasons and Climate
The seasons on Ephyrea are 2 months long and are caused by the distance to [[θ]]. [[θ]] Follows an elliptical orbit resulting in warmer and colder periods during the year. The year starts and ends during winter, then spring, summer and autumn. The seasons are divided in perfect two month periods but the true seasons are not equal in length. Winter is the longest and creeps up early in autumn and lingers during spring. True summer is the shortest as days shorten fast and [[ψ]] climbs slow. The climate on Ephyrea is the most extreme in the frozen outskirts of Galdron and the scorching deserts of [[Hadarac]]. But in between the climate can vary immensely. There is much arable soil on most of the continents with [[Kovir]] being the exception.

### Population, people and languages
The total population of Ephyrea is estimated to be around XXX inhabitants, however, many regions remain unexplored or too secretive to allow for an accurate accounting. The largest realms by population are [[The Nelorian Empire]], [[Treonia]] & [[Tamor]]. 

Ephyrea is home to a large number of languages and dialects, but since the unification of the continents, great value has been placed on the common tongue. Narius (the common tongue) is an ancient language that is the progenitor of most languages in Ephyrea. Narius is the official language used by the [[E.R.C.]] and thus being proficient or having a translator is incredible important for intercontinental interactions. Each continent also has their own common language. This can be a dialect of one of the more prominent languages or a sub-progenitor on that continent. 

Common languages per continent:
- **Lothria**: Treonian. The language of the largest city on Ephyrea has found itself to become quite influential, however within the city proper Narius is also commonly spoken.
- **Irrida**: Ridian is the progenitor of most languages on [[Irrida]] and is quite similar to the language spoken in [[Tamor]].
- Thera: [[The Nelorian Empire]] quickly instated Nelorian as the official language of [[Thera]] after starting its unifying conquest.
- Kovir: Is the only continent without a true common tongue. Both [[Galdron]] and [[Cindore]] have their own languages.
