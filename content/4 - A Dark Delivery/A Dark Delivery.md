## Chapter 1 - Exposition

There is always work within [[The Nelorian Empire | The Empire]], everyone can find something to do that fits their capabilities. Whatever the trade or craft, there is always someone looking for it. But of course such a grand empire like [[Nelore]] doesn't sustain itself without a bit of organisation and structure. So there are many imperial companies and guilds, who provide services all  throughout [[The Nelorian Empire | The Empire]]. These guilds and companies provide plenty of work for those seeking. The city of [[Weldar]], being located so close to the only intercontinental trade route is, of course well suited for those looking for work. Many mercenaries, hirelings and adventures reside there from time to time. Many guilds and companies frequently give notice for jobs that require external hirings. One such company is [[Raven Wares]], who recently posted one such a notice.

***Imperial Notice of Contracted Employment***

*By Decree of Raven Wares, Licensed Merchant Consortium of The Empire*

*Let it be known that Raven Wares, duly registered under the Imperial Charter of Trade and recognized by the Guild of Commerce, seeks a discreet and capable party of three to five for the guarded overland conveyance of valuable cargo.*

*Secure transportation of company property is required, from the city-hold of Weldar to the bastion of Ederan. Conveyance shall be via wagon, provisioned by Raven Wares. Route of travel shall be left to the discretion of the hirelings, under two strictures: Cities are to be avoided. The goods must arrive within fourteen nights after departure. Expedient completion will be rewarded.*

*Compensation:*
- *150 gp, paid in advance upon contract binding*
- *450 gp, upon verified delivery at Ederan*
- *Expedition bonus: 15 gp per day delivered ahead of schedule*

*Interested parties may present themselves at the Raven Wares trade office in Weldar. Only those of proven discretion and loyalty to The Empire need apply.*

***By Order of the Raven***

*May commerce be ever in your favor.*
