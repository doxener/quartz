### Description
Almar Kreston was a long standing member of the hunters guild in [[Ridgeview]]. He earned his place and respect under Greathunter Ferrick Nort and his responsibilities were to often lead  hunts or important traveling parties through the [[Whisperstone mountains]]. 

On one of his expeditions in the Whisperstone mountains - He led a group of scouts through the mountain range searching for signs of [[Hadarac]] forces. This expedition started on XX-XX-XXXX about one month before the [[Grallo]] collapse. He and his companions were ambushed by a pair of basilisks. Since they weren't seen here before they were quite surprised and quickly all turned to stone.
