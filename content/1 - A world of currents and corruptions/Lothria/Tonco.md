Tonco is a soft metal alloy created by the dwarves of [[Fyor-Grad]] that has the unique quality of conducting light. It allowed them to bring daylight deep within the caverns and mines of [[Farthen-Dur]]. Tonco is the biggest export of [[Fyor-Grad]] and is widely used throughout [[Ephyrea]] to bring light into dark spaces.

Recently there have been studies and experiments started looking into the possibility of Tonco for communicating over long distances.
