The city of Ridgeview lies on the northern edge of the [[Whisperstone Mountains]] and is one of the largest cities in Eastern-[[Tamor]]. As of 1345 AS Ridgeview has become the landbound military centre of [[Tamor]] since the war with [[Hadarac]]. Ridgeview is believed to be one of the oldest settlements of civilisation on [[Irrida]] from after [[The Shattering]]. The city itself is devised from three large caverns on top of each other inside Mount Tour. The three caverns: Perdu, Marbor and Ramond house a combined populous of [    ] citizens.

## History
Ridgeview, although not always known by that name, is believed to be one of the oldest settlements of [[Irrida]] after [[The Shattering]]. The three large caverns provided the shelter needed for people to survive the years after the shattering and start [[the Re-Founding]] on [[Irrida]]. The meltwater from out of the [[Whisperstone Mountains]] brought fresh drinkable water inside the caverns and the surrounding soil and climate were suitable for both agriculture and livestock.

The settlement never really grew to the size of a city because of the limited daylight that the caverns received. The lands surrounding Ridgeview were always in use for livestock or agriculture so the the caverns were always a place were you could find trade or protection. Not until after the creation of the [[E.R.C.]] and the introduction of [[Tonco]] from [[Fyor-Grad]] to [[Irrida]] that Ridgeview actually began to grow. 

The central government of [[Tamor]] was spearheading the development of Ridgeview. Tamor was always looking for a more fortified position in their eastern territories. They always saw the potential of Ridgeview but it had its limitations. When [[Tonco]] was introduced in [[Irrida]], [[Earron Raltara]], at the time sovereign of Tamor, struck a deal with the engineers of [[Fyor-Grad]] to bring light to Ridgeview. 

The development was quite successful. Ridgeview became a connection point for landbound trade, from southern [[Irrida]] to Tamor and other regions. In addition, as was planned, Ridgeview became a formidable bastion for Tamor.

## Lore & Legends

#### Ancient Crumblers
Some still believe in the old rumours, but most think of them as old tales without any truth to them, or they hope for them to be true, to not feel trapped, this deep within the mountain. The tale goes that that when [[Tamor]] and [[Fyor-Grad]] worked together on the construction of this bastion city, that when it was complete the High Thane [[Halrikk Kazadur]] gifted [[Iliar Toldir]], the lord of Ridgeview, two young [[Crumblers]], as a token of their friendship. 

They were believed to be kept deep within the lowest bowels of Ridgeview where they were thought to listen to human commands rather than ancient dwarvish. Some say that they only listen to the bloodline of Toldir, while others believe that it was just a tool of manipulation by the later descendants. 

They were meant as either a tool for expansion or a way of escaping, but since nobody has seen them in centuries they are mostly considered myths. However since [[Crumblers]] can in theory live an indefinite, suspended life in hibernation, some believe they're still down there awaiting their next command.
