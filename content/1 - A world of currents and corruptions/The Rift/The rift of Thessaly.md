The rift of Thessaly also known as the dark rift is what has defined [[Ephyrea]] after the shattering. At the heart of the rift lies the stellar remnant of [[Thessaly]].  The rift is not vacuous space as the space surrounding the world but is filled with strange elements and components some of which are only found inside the rift. The rift is famous for its currents. Strange elements interact and create forces that affect all civilizations on [[Ephyrea]]. The rift is also home to a number of unique and strange creatures that have found their place in this dark expanse. Thessaly also holds numerous notable locations that have sparked the interest of people and creatures alike.
