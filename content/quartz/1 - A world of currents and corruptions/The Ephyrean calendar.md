The calendar in Ephyrea is based on the planetary cycles. One cycle takes 105 years. A year lasts 384 days, divided into 8 months, each lasting 12 weeks. The 4 days of the week are called: Brigthday, Duskday, Darkday and Dawnday. The days are 24 hours long, weeks are a 4 day cycle, a month is 12 weeks and a year is 8 moths, one cycle is 105 years.

#### The days on Ephyrea
Days on Ephyrea are measured by the passing of [[ψ]] over the continents. [[ψ]] is the only major rift moon that follows a stable path. [[ψ]] orbits through the rift and circulation rotating around the different continents, thus resulting in a crossed lemniscate orbit. The four loops create the four distinct days of the week on Ephyrea. The first, **Brigthday**, is the day that [[ψ]] passes directly over the continent resulting in 12 hours of daylight. The second day, **Duskday**, [[ψ]] passes over the continent to the east, thus only giving 6 hours of light just above the northern horizon. The third, **Darkday**, [[ψ]] is on the opposite side of the rift, casting the continent in 24 hours of darkness. The last day, **Dawnday**, is when [[ψ]] is above the continent to the west, resulting in 6 hours of light above the southern horizon.

####  The planetary cycles
The planetary orbits within [[The Pithia System]] seem to have unnaturally balanced intervals that astronomers to this day still only have unproven theories for, mostly revolving around the systemics of [[Elements from the rift#Darkness|Darkness]] and [[Elements from the rift#Rift Light|Intent]] on a macro scale. The closest planet, [[α]], completes a full orbit in 48 days, this timeframe has been called a month and is used to sub-divide the year, which is marked by the moment the furthest planet, [[θ]], completes an orbit and is eclipsed by [[α]], marking the beginning of the next year. Every other planet takes 48 days longer to complete an orbit than the planet before.

###### True Convergence
These planetary orbits in systemic intervals create a shifting pattern convergences, where multiple planets can be seen in the in the sky at the same time. Most notable the true convergence, where all 8 planets can be seen at the same time. This convergence only happens once every 105 years. The rarity of this occasion has turned this event into one of the most important events throughout Ephyrea. It is surrounded by plenty superstition but also by proven affects on the natural world, most notably on certain types of magic. 

###### False Convergence
The false convergence happens when all but one planet are visibly in the sky. The planet [[η]] is the only planet that wont be visible, thus disrupting true convergences every 15 years (6 times per cycle). For this reason [[η]] has been seen as a sign of bad luck and an omen for unfortunate events. Between the superstition and rumours are also observable affects caused by [[η]] and the false convergence. There seems to be an overlap between the birth of Tieflings/Omens and concievement when [[η]] was visible in the sky.

###### Other Convergences
The influence of the planets is strong Ephyrea and every bit of precious light that is introduced to to the sky seems to affect the world and forces within. Some of these effects have been theorized and proven, like the relation between schools of magic and visible planets, and others are still to be discovered.

#### Notations
The most common way of describing the date is dd/mm/yy/cc. Our story takes place 90 years in the 21st cycle (21c90 / 2295 A.S.), So a date notation could be 27/12/90/21. A more official way of notating the year is the archivist's notation which was created in the [[[ ] archive]], as a mechanical way of giving the year a unique key with only 25 variables. The notations is based on which planets are visible in which month, thus marking a year in the cycle, then the cycle is marked by a dashed line on the number of the cycle.
