
#### Darkness

Darkness is the main component of the rift. It is a gas that acts similarly to air in the atmosphere, with a few exceptions. Mainly the gas greatly dims any light that travels through it, hence the name. Secondly even though it acts similar to air, organisms from [[Ephyrea]] cannot breathe through it, even though it does contain oxygen.

Darkness can also enter the atmosphere. A few famous places throughout [[Ephyrea]] with a high darkness concentration are: [[Dras-Leona]], [[The Nixian Maw]], [[The shaded Cliffs]] and [[The Galdron Outskirts]].

#### Rift Light

This component reacts with it's surroundings, creating a directional field resulting in an invisible force called intent. Intent in high concentration creates the famous purple shimmering lights seen all throughout the rift.

Rift light can be harvested from the rift, or high darkness concentration areas, with the right magnetic field. A magnetic probe will collect large quantities over time. The rift light can be fused together and crystallized in the right orientation with enough energy and a proper directional field.

The harvesting of rift light has been exclusively been done by the [[E.R.C.]] for centuries. They have patented and kept their methods a secret, but recently the republic of [[Anor]] has figured out there own way to harvest and process rift light.

#### Stygium

Stygium is a small silver coloured component that floats through the rift. In darkness it is an imperceptible gas but once it freezes under extreme temperatures, it solidifies into a fine silver sand. Once solidified it is really hard to turn it into a gas again. 

This silver sand can be found on [[The shimmering Coast]] in [[Kovir]]. There it is gathered and processed. With enough heating the sand can be turned into a strange steel like fibre. This fibre can be incorporated in many different materials. Most famously [[Stygian Sails]] which are used for traversing the the [[torrents]] and [[currents]] maintained by the [[E.R.C.]] 

#### Inertium

Inertium is most commonly used in rift anchors. Naturally it is found floating stationary in the belt of [[Thera]].  This is its main and most important characteristic, it stays stationary when in contact with darkness. Inertium looks like  rust brown red Vulcanic glass and once it is exposed to darkness it will react and glow dimly and a light vapour start to emanate from it. 

[[Nelorians]] first discovered the component and its properties. They managed to harvest it on their eastern coast by enclosing it within clay so it would not react with darkness. 

#### Cinderstone

Cinderstone is a porous, brittle and brown stone, that is a residuum left over by certain meteorites. The stone has glowing specks like embers from a flame. Once crumbled cinders escape violently and evaporate. 

These cinders have special qualities, when the stone is crumbled in darkness the cinders and vapours have a magic dampening effect. To prolong these effects the cinders are trapped within glass. Most famously the painted windows of [[The Hagia Leona]]. 

When the Cinderstone is crumbled in air the cinders have a magic enhancing effect. This again can be sustained by trapping it in glass, most famously applied in the [[Academy of Ithral]]. The only downside of the vapours are that they have a soothing but highly addictive effect. For this reason Cinderstone has been adopted as a popular drug called [[Crush]]. Cinderstone is also used for the cultivation of [[Crush#Heat Herbs|Heat Herbs]]. 

Cinderstone was first found on [[Cindore]] but their supplies have gone dry since the use of it throughout [[Ithral]] by their many mages. Nowadays it is only found, although in large quantities, throughout the ruined lands of [[Y'Dris]].

#### Dark Steel

The intent in the rift also affects certain metals. The energy and directional force, restructure and orientate the molecules resulting in the metal called *Dark Steel*. The metal itself shows strange patterns that reflect light differently, but overall the metal absorbs more light than it reflects, resulting in its name. 

The metal is quite rare but can be found in meteorites in all four continents. It is a more common occurrence in the rift itself. [[Anor]] has hopes of directly mining it from within the rift. The metal is a highly sought after material for armour and weaponry all throughout [[Ephyrea]]. Individuals or organisations with plenty to spend are usually those who regularly use it.  

#### Resolve

Resolve is a mostly stationary gas that can be found in the rift. Resolve famously is in high concentration in front of [[Anor]]. The peninsula used to be a [[Dark Drift]] but collided with mainland [[Irrida]] not long after [[The Shattering]]. This resulted in the only large scale resolve deposit found on [[Ephyrea]], where the tectonics collided within a resolve cloud. The properties of resolve have only recently been discovered by scientist in [[Anor]]. 

Resolve absorbs kinetic energy in collisions which turns the gas’s into solid crystals. The energy stored in these crystals can be extracted thus turning them into a power source. Using the crystals unrefined turns the centre of the crystal to gas. This builds up pressure and can cause incredibly violent explosions. Once the crystals are properly refined and stabilized the outside will release gas first, making them safe to use. 

[[Anor]] is the only country that has access to large quantities of resolve. Therefore they are the only ones who know of its properties and how to use it. They have used resolve to massively progress their technology. Their most notable creations involving resolve are automatons, weapons and [[Rift Liners]].
