### Traits
**_Beastly Control_**  
The warg has a telepathic link with a number of beasts determined by his skill and experience. The warg can command these beasts to do his bidding requiring no action from the warg.

**_A Wild Escape_**  
When the warg is reduced to 0 hit points and has an animal under its control, the warg can make a DC 14 Wis save to transfer its consciousness to the animal companion and take complete control.  
Staying in this from requires successive WIS saves to maintain control and maintain sanity. The warg keeps its mental stats and still understands all the languages it knew. In addition the warg still has acces to the wild escape feature.
